Welcome to Leamokgetswe Old Age Centre

Safety, Care, Community - A Home Away From Home

We provide 24-hour loving care for 65 elderly residents in Greater Letaba. Our mission is to ensure every gogo and mkhulu lives with dignity, comfort, and joy.

Daily activities, prayer, birthdays, and family visit days to fight loneliness.
