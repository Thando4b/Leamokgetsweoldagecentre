About Leamokgetswe Old Age Centre

Who We Are

Leamokgetswe Old Age Centre is a community-based non-profit organisation dedicated to supporting elderly people in our community.

Our centre provides a safe, caring and welcoming environment where older people can participate in social activities, community programmes and other support services.

Our History

Leamokgetswe Old Age Centre was established to provide support and companionship to elderly members of the community. The centre works together with families, volunteers and community members to improve the quality of life of older people.

Through our programmes and community activities, we aim to reduce social isolation and create opportunities for elderly people to remain connected with their community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide elderly people with a safe, respectful and supportive environment that promotes dignity, social connection and wellbeing.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a caring community where older people are respected, supported and actively included in society.

Our Values

  • Respect
  • Dignity
  • Compassion
  • Community
  • Inclusivity
  • Care

Who We Serve

Our services are aimed primarily at elderly people in the local community. We also work with families, caregivers, volunteers, donors and community organisations.
