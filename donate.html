Support Our Elders - Donate With Love

Your donation provides meals, medical care, and companionship for 65 elderly residents at our centre. Many have no family support.

R100 - Daily Meals

Provides 3 nutritious meals + tea for one elder for a day. Includes special diabetic and soft meals.

R250 - Medical Care

Covers blood pressure checks, medication, and transport to clinic for one elder for a month.

R600 - Full Care Sponsor

Sponsors full accommodation, meals, laundry and 24hr care for one elder for a week.
