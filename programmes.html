Our Care Programmes

We provide holistic care to ensure our elders live with dignity, comfort and joy.

1. 24-Hour Nursing & Health Care Programme

What we do: Daily health monitoring, medication management, wound care, and monthly doctor visits. Physiotherapy twice a week.

Impact: 65 elders receive daily care. 90% have improved blood pressure control.

2. Nutrition & Wellness Programme

What we do: 3 balanced meals daily prepared by our kitchen. Special diets for diabetic, high blood and soft foods. Weekly exercise and storytelling sessions.

3. Social & Spiritual Support Programme

What we do: Daily prayer, Sunday church service, birthday celebrations, family visit days, arts & crafts, and memory games to prevent loneliness.
